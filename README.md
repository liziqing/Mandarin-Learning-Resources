## 💡 Tips & Notes

A place to jot down learning tips, personal notes, and things that clicked.

- **Tones matter** — Mandarin has 4 tones + a neutral tone; getting these right early makes a big difference.
- **Learn Pinyin first** — Pinyin is the romanisation system for Mandarin; it's the foundation for pronunciation.
- **Consistency beats intensity** — Short daily sessions beat long infrequent ones, especially for character memorisation.
- *Add your own tips here as you learn!*
